How it works

One request. Coordinated agents. Verified results.

Start with a simple workflow—like an inbound customer call—and connect the departments involved.

1. Capture

A call, email or business-system event creates an authorized task.

2. Route

The command center assigns work to specialist agents.

3. Execute

Agents use permitted tools to draft, check and prepare actions.

4. Approve & Record

People approve sensitive decisions; activity is tracked for review.
