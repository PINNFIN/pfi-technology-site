Getting started

Connect a compatible USB drive to your iPhone or iPad, select the USB folder, choose your player profile and scan.

Safe repair

Back up important music before testing. Keep the drive connected during repairs. Preserve Quality supports lossless correction of eligible WAV headers; other conversions create working copies. If a recovery original is retained, export it before further direct repairs.

Reporting a problem

Include your app build, iOS version, USB format, player model and error message. Do not send private music files unless necessary and authorized.

Compatibility scans do not guarantee playback or playlist database integrity. Verify on your intended DJ hardware.
