Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026.

Local processing

GigReady processes audio files locally on your device. It accesses folders and files you select to scan, diagnose and perform supported repairs. Direct USB replacement requires your approval.

Data collection and tracking

The current iOS app does not transmit your music library to PFI Technologies, require an account, or use your audio for advertising or tracking.

Storage and sharing

Working copies and temporary backups may be stored locally. Recovery originals may remain after an interrupted or unverified repair. You can share reports and files using the device sharing interface at your direction. Recipients and services you choose have their own privacy practices.

Support and website

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Website hosting may process ordinary connection information. This policy describes the GigReady iOS app; other PFI website pages may use separate analytics.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email info@pfitechnology.com.
