The CYQOR mission

Protect intelligence. Protect systems. Build trust.

As AI agents gain access to operational data, applications, and infrastructure, new security controls are needed to distinguish authorized work from malicious instructions, unsafe access, and unexpected system behavior.

CYQOR's mission is to translate established scientific research, standards, and validated defensive methods into accountable security technology. Our goal is not another dashboard. It is independently testable protection logic, with clear evidence of what works and where limitations remain.

Research before claims. CYQOR is in the research and architecture phase. The proposed capabilities described here are development objectives, not deployed or certified security services.
Unified defense architecture

Four coordinated engines. One security foundation.

CYQOR is designed around a shared policy, evidence, and monitoring layer rather than four disconnected products.

ENGINE 01 / AI SECURITY

AI Inference & Agent Defense

Research controls for prompt-injection resilience, authorized tool use, sensitive-data boundaries, and supervised autonomous workflows.

Research target: policy enforcement with measurable agent-security benchmarks
ENGINE 02 / QUANTUM READINESS

Post-Quantum Security

Inventory cryptographic dependencies, assess exposure, and plan migration to vetted, standardized post-quantum methods.

Research target: evidence-backed cryptographic migration assessments
ENGINE 03 / CYBER INTELLIGENCE

Cyber Threat & Risk Intelligence

Organize security events, threat indicators, vulnerability findings, and investigation evidence for authorized defensive analysis.

Research target: explainable, testable detection and triage logic
ENGINE 04 / RESILIENCE

Critical Infrastructure Resilience

Research defensive monitoring, segmentation, failure recovery, and operational-risk visibility for sensitive environments.

Research target: controlled laboratory evaluations before operational use
Research-to-engineering

From published research to verifiable controls.

We intend to prioritize reproducible results over speculative capabilities, building each method through transparent engineering stages.

01 / RESEARCH

Establish the evidence

Evaluate peer-reviewed studies, NIST guidance, established threat models, and licensing constraints.

02 / IMPLEMENT

Translate into logic

Develop deterministic policies, detection methods, and documented assumptions using approved software components.

03 / TEST

Run controlled experiments

Use synthetic data, authorized test agents, and reproducible baselines to quantify outcomes.

04 / VALIDATE

Invite independent review

Seek qualified assessment of security performance, failure modes, and readiness for further development.

Technical merit first

Security claims must be earned.

Our intended validation criteria are designed to make engineering progress inspectable by security specialists, academic researchers, and future partners.

CYQOR will begin with a narrow defensive use case: evaluating whether AI agents follow authorized instructions and resist malicious content in controlled environments.

01Citable scientific foundations for every proposed control
02Working implementations rather than presentation-only demonstrations
03Detection, false-positive, performance and failure measurements
04Repeatable tests, baselines, logged evidence and independent review
