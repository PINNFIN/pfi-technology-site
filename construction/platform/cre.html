Calculation Controls & Underwriting Limitations

This model uses a fictional 120-unit deal. It does not perform property tax reassessment, discounted rent roll analysis, lease-by-lease rollover, tax depreciation, lender covenants, complex refinance, layered investor catch-up/clawback, asset management fees, sale taxes or actual XIRR dated to irregular transactions.
