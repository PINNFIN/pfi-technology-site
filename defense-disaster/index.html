How PFI FieldVision works together.

One integrated software platform, installed on a wearable PFI computing device and connected to approved field hardware. Each function shares a single authenticated operator session, local case record, and evidence trail.

AR GlassesSee maps, imagery, alerts and results
Voice & GesturesHands-free interaction and object selection
PFI Pocket ComputerLocal AI, secure data, workflows and power
Drone & SensorsAuthorized imagery, telemetry and GPS
Verified OutcomesEvidence, reports and reviewed actions

The mini computer runs a unified field application and local services. Approved drone and sensor adapters feed it imagery and coordinates; AR glasses display the operator interface. Data can be saved locally when disconnected and securely synchronized when connectivity returns. Flight-critical functions remain with qualified operators and approved controllers. Hardware compatibility, battery endurance and offline performance require prototype validation.

Real-world scenario

From a hurricane assessment to a documented outcome.

1. Observe & Locate

An authorized operator gathers drone imagery and field observations; the platform records available coordinates and timestamps.

2. Assess & Prioritize

Local AI flags possible damage and organizes supporting evidence for responder verification and triage.

3. Deliver & Coordinate

PFI prepares a location-tagged damage summary and recovery recommendations, ready for human review and sharing with authorized teams.

Development roadmap

Designed for real deployment - validated in stages.

Connect wearable AR and gesture input; validate the mini computer and power system; integrate simulated drone video and location data; connect compatible inspection hardware under human control; then test secure reporting, offline operation and field reliability.

Development status: PFI FieldVision™ and the described modules are proposed capabilities under development, not currently deployed or certified disaster response or defense systems. Mention of FEMA, state agencies, defense contractors or relief organizations identifies potential users, not endorsements, contracts or partnerships. Live flights, safety-critical findings and operational decisions require authorized professionals and appropriate regulatory compliance.
